# Code of Conduct

PK-Stack contributors are expected to make participation safe, respectful, and
useful for everyone.

Examples of welcome behavior include:

- assuming good intent while discussing evidence and trade-offs;
- being specific, constructive, and open to correction;
- respecting different backgrounds, identities, and levels of experience; and
- protecting private data, credentials, and unpublished work.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, intimidation,
threats, sexualized conduct, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and publishing
someone else's private information. Technical disagreement is welcome; personal
attacks are not.

Raise conduct concerns privately through the repository's GitHub reporting
channel or a maintainer. Reports will be handled discreetly and fairly. A
maintainer may remove content, pause participation, or take another action
needed to protect the community.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1.
