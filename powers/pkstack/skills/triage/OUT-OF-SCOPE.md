# Retain rejection decisions in existing project knowledge

Use the existing Wiki topic for the concept and the OKF document lifecycle. Do not introduce
`.out-of-scope/` or another knowledge tree. Match prior requests by meaning, not title alone.

An accepted enhancement rejection can be retained with the decision, durable reason, scope,
sources, and related issue/PR links. Update an existing concept entry idempotently. A temporary
capacity limit is a deferral; already implemented behavior is a feature fact, not a rejection.
A bug that remains unfixed is not rewritten as an accepted requirement merely because it closed.

Surface a matching decision with its reason and current relevance. Reuse settled authority rather
than routinely asking for confirmation. If new evidence warrants reconsideration, mark the decision
superseded through the existing lifecycle, retaining its history and source links. Capturing or
revising knowledge does not authorize tracker comments, closure, or reopening. In read-only modes,
keep the proposed knowledge update conversational and report that retention is pending.
