# PKStack ghost friends — three logo options

Concepts generated on 2026-09-06 with the built-in image generation tool. The previous logo and banner were used as visual references. The user selected **Option 2: Knowledge crest**. Its PNG is copied unchanged to the Power's `assets/logo.png` and displayed in both READMEs. The other options remain here for reference.

All three use four separate ghosts: pale Kiro with the graduation cap; golden Poteto; a coral HumanLayer interpretation with overlapping layers; and a blue TypeScript ghost with a smooth bald crown, round glasses, and a TS badge. The TypeScript character is a generic archetype, not a portrait. The HumanLayer treatment is a proposed visual metaphor. The linked-node tree and book carry the OKF knowledge theme.

## Option 1: Study group

[Open image](option-1-study-group.png)

Tactile 3D group gathered around a knowledge book; closest to the original artwork.

### Final prompt

```text
Use case: logo-brand.
Create a polished square PKStack logo concept featuring EXACTLY FOUR distinct friendly ghost characters: Kiro as the lead ghost, plus three separate ghost friends representing Poteto, HumanLayer, and a generic TypeScript expert.
Input image 1 is the CURRENT LOGO: use as reference for the appealing mascot quality, purple/navy/cyan/gold palette, and navy graduation cap with cyan tassel. Redesign the composition; do not keep all identities fused into a potato.
Input image 2 is the CURRENT BANNER: use as reference for the dark navy atmosphere and branching connected-node KNOWLEDGE TREE motif, which represents OKF knowledge.
Character identities: Kiro is a clean pearly white/lavender ghost with two dark oval eyes, and wears the navy graduation cap with cyan tassel. Poteto is a separate warm golden potato ghost with a few subtle skin speckles/dimples and dark oval eyes. HumanLayer is a separate coral-orange ghost with three subtly offset translucent layers along its lower silhouette, a visual cue for human layers. The TypeScript friend is a separate blue ghost with a completely smooth bald rounded crown, bold round dark glasses, friendly eyes visible through the glasses, and a small crisp white "TS" badge. This is a generic ghost archetype, not a portrait of Matt Pocock: no human skin, nose, beard, ears, or human body. Every character must unmistakably be a ghost with a rounded floating body and a simple scalloped lower edge.
Keep the graduation hat and visible branching linked-node knowledge symbolism. Give each ghost its own readable silhouette. No extra characters, no extra faces, no humanoid person, no watermark, no mockup surface, no collage of options.
Option 1 — STUDY GROUP. Premium soft 3D mascot rendering, closest to the existing logo's tactile style. On a deep violet rounded-square app-icon tile, arrange the four ghosts in a tight friendly semicircle around a small open navy knowledge book. Kiro is tallest at the back center, clearly its own pearly ghost under the graduation hat; potato left, layered coral friend lower center, blue glasses friend right. All four faces fully visible and unobstructed. From the open book rise a restrained few cyan linked nodes forming a small knowledge tree between the characters; leave faces clean. Warm, charming, gently tactile surfaces, soft contact shadows, subtle cyan rim light, strong dimensional shapes. Fill the square confidently with generous safe margin. Icon only, no wordmark or caption; the only text is the tiny "TS" badge.
```
